A working proof-of-concept for the Global Quranic Concordance Platform. Pick a concept below — the app fetches the Arabic ayah and a classical tafsir live from open Quran/Tafsir APIs, then drafts a translation from the tafsir's meaning, not from an existing translation.

1 · Concept→ 2 · Ayah (live, Uthmani script)→ 3 · Tafsir (live, classical source)→ 4 · Draft translation of the tafsir→ 5 · Scholar review (pending)
Data sourcing, live at request time: Arabic text via api.alquran.cloud (Uthmani script) · Tafsir via spa5k/tafsir_api (Tafsir Ibn Kathir, abridged English) · Draft translation via MyMemory Translation Memory. Concept-to-ayah mappings in data/concepts.json are the seed of your father's original index — this prototype ships with 5 sample concepts; the production build migrates the full Malayalam original. Nothing here is theologically final: every card marked pending review is a machine draft awaiting a scholar, by design.
